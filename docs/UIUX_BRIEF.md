# UI/UX BRIEF
Direction: calm mission-planning tool. Light warm-grey background, one dark text colour, IBM Plex Sans with Source Serif 4 headings, tabular numbers. Tokens are CSS variables at the top of app/style.css.
Colour: teal = match, amber = partial, brick red hatched = gap, grey = no qualifying candidate. Colour is always paired with hatch, dots or text (month cells carry a number and a symbol).
Layout: header (name, Quick tour, tabs grouped Plan / Compare / Evidence / About); map on the left with the demo bar and legend; one panel on the right. Phone: map on top, demo bar and legend under it, panel below, tabs scroll sideways.
Panel order: 1 Terrain to copy, 2 What your test needs, 3 Map options, terrain match card, result (Fit Verdict, month strip, metrics, slope chart, twin pictures, context rows, limits box), share link.
Quick tour: 9 steps (what it does, target, test needs, map colours, month strip, Fit Verdict and limits, twin pictures, demo/brief/share, Evidence tabs). Offered once; button always in the header; Esc closes.
Print: Test Site Brief keeps its own white one-page style.
