# UI/UX BRIEF
Direction: dark space theme, deep navy to black with two soft glows, fine star field on the landing page. IBM Plex Sans for text and numbers, Space Grotesk for headings. Tokens are CSS variables at the top of app/style.css.
Colour: teal = match, amber = partial, brick red hatched = gap, grey = no qualifying candidate. Colour is always paired with hatch, dots or text.
Flow: landing page (Start planning, Quick tour, five page tiles) then the app. Top bar: logo (back to landing), Planner, World, Evidence, About, More (Gap view, Calendar, Fine-scale checks, Click Mars / Moon, Name-free check, AMADEE-18 replay), Try an example, Quick tour.
Planner: map opens on the world (45 chosen places as dots, Oman as the fine-detail area; click Oman to zoom in). Panel: 1 Target, 2 Test (presets, one sentence; Advanced needs and Map options collapsed), 3 Result (best-square card, Fit Verdict, month strip, then collapsed sections: numbers and context, twin pictures and 3D, access and geology, confidence, what this cannot see).
Phone: map on top (46% of height), one step at a time with a fixed bottom bar (Back / Next). Selecting a square jumps to step 3.
Long intro text keeps its first sentence; the rest is under "More". Hard terms have "?" hints.
Motion: slow drift of the planets, star parallax, short fades. Respects reduced motion; the sphere animation stops when the tab is hidden or the landing page is closed.
Print: Test Site Brief keeps its own white one-page style.
