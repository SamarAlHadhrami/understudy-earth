# App flow

## Start
A landing page offers Start planning and a Quick tour. The top bar has Planner, World, Evidence, Calendar, About and a More menu, plus Try an example and Quick tour. On a phone the planner is three steps: Target, Test, Result.

## Planner
1. **Target:** choose a Moon or Mars place. The map opens on the world; click the large Oman dot to zoom to the best Oman square and open its card.
2. **Test:** press a preset (Rover, Instrument, Walking, Cold) or type a sentence and press Apply. Advanced needs and Map options sit below. Unknown sentences are refused with a message.
3. **Result:** the panel jumps to the Result block (it shows the match badge). The map shows the squares.

## Result card
Clicking a square (or a world dot, or a Mars or Moon spot) opens a card over the map, or a bottom sheet on a phone. It shows a one-line verdict, the 12-month strip, and buttons: Test site brief, Add to calendar, Compare, Compare terrain pictures, 3D view, Open in Google Maps (a plain link to a satellite map). Compare opens a side-by-side table of two squares. Details hold the fit weights, numbers, pictures, surface material proxy, access, geology, confidence and limits.

## Other pages
- **World:** 45 chosen places coloured for the current target.
- **Evidence:** baseline, name-free check, gap view and the AMADEE-18 replay, as four cards.
- **Calendar:** chosen squares by month, with an optional two-site plan.
- **More:** click or type coordinates on Mars (87 S to 87 N) and the lunar poles; fine-scale Jezero and Gale checks; About with how it works, checks and credits.
- Every page except Planner has a Back to Planner button.

## Examples and sharing
Try an example opens a good match, a no-good-match state or a Test Site Brief. The share link stores the target and needs in the address.
